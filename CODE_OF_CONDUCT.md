# Code of Conduct

## Our expectations

Render Awake welcomes participation regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. Help keep issues, pull requests, and other project spaces respectful and useful.

- Be considerate and use inclusive language.
- Discuss technical ideas constructively; critique the work rather than the person.
- Assume good intent where reasonable, ask clarifying questions, and accept corrections.
- Respect differing experience levels, personal boundaries, and privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing someone’s private information without permission are unacceptable. Repeated disruptive behavior or retaliation against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to **Ayush Raj** at [ayush_24a12res897@iitp.ac.in](mailto:ayush_24a12res897@iitp.ac.in). Include the relevant context and links or evidence when safe to share. Do not post private details in a public issue.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible, with information shared only as needed to assess and address the concern. The maintainer will respect the reporter’s privacy and avoid unnecessary disclosure.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for applying this policy fairly and considering the context and severity of a report. Responses may include requesting a correction, issuing a warning, removing inappropriate content, or temporarily or permanently restricting participation. Concerns about an enforcement decision can be sent to the same private contact for reconsideration.

This original policy applies in project spaces and when someone explicitly represents Render Awake in public.
